Master's thesis (in progress)

How do recruitment professionals navigate and make sense of an efficiency-driven working life in which AI increases the pace of work?

The thesis studies how recruiters themselves experience AI. It sets two expectations against each other. The first is the efficiency promise: AI takes over routine work and frees up time for conversations, judgement and relationships. The second comes from acceleration theory: the freed-up time is absorbed by more tasks and a higher pace, so the work becomes faster but emptier. The aim is to identify when each of these happens.

Theory

  • Fast & Flow (Utility Time vs. Sense Time)
  • Hartmut Rosa: acceleration and resonance
  • Karl Weick: sensemaking
  • Karl Marx: alienation

Method and relevance

Qualitative interviews with recruitment professionals. The findings are meant to help HR and leaders introduce AI deliberately, with attention to well-being, retention and professional quality. At its core, this is a change management question.
